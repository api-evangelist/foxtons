# Foxtons

> Foxtons Group plc is a London-focused UK estate agency, founded in 1981 and listed on the London Stock Exchange, running residential sales, lettings and property management from roughly 40 branches across London and Surrey, plus mortgage broking through its Alexander Hall subsidiary. It publishes **no API**. There is no developer portal, no OpenAPI, OData, GraphQL or AsyncAPI contract, no SDK, no webhook catalogue and no partner programme. The only machine-readable surface Foxtons serves is schema.org JSON-LD embedded in its web pages.

This file is generated by API Evangelist from the Foxtons company profile; Foxtons does not publish an llms.txt of its own (https://www.foxtons.co.uk/llms.txt returns 404).

## What an agent should know first

- There is nothing to sign up for. Foxtons has no API keys, no OAuth clients, no scopes and no API terms of service. The barrier is absence, not credentials.
- `api.foxtons.co.uk`, `developer.foxtons.co.uk` and `docs.foxtons.co.uk` do **not resolve** (NXDOMAIN). Search results citing `api.foxtons.co.uk` URLs are stale index artifacts and are not evidence of an API.
- `robots.txt` disallows `/api/` and `/json/`. Those are internal AJAX paths behind the website; both return 404 at their roots, neither is documented or offered, and treating them as an interface would be a mistake.
- Foxtons' business runs on BOS, an internally built and internally managed CRM/ERP. The posture is deliberately proprietary-integrated, not platform-extensible.
- Listing distribution reaches consumers through Rightmove and Zoopla over the UK's portal feed arrangements. The UK has no MLS, so there is no RESO certification and no cooperative data standard to call.

## Where the open UK property data actually is

The genuinely open, callable UK property data layer is public-sector, not agency-published: HM Land Registry Price Paid and ownership data under the Open Government Licence, and Ordnance Survey open addressing products (OS Open UPRN). An agent looking for machine-readable UK property data should start there. Foxtons is a consumer of that layer, not a publisher of it.
